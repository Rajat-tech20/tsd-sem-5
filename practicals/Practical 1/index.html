Our Story

Three generations,
one banana leaf.

Ilai Thali began as a single stove in a Mylapore side-street, where our grandmother fed mill workers on leaves cut fresh from her own backyard. The recipes haven't moved since — idlis steamed in the old cloth-lined baskets, sambar built on a tamarind base that simmers for six hours, and a filter coffee ritual that isn't rushed for anyone.

We still serve on banana leaf. Not for the photograph — because rice tastes different when it's eaten off one, and because that's how a thali is meant to be shared.
